# Starter topics — PUBLISHED Nov 20 2026 (approved by Timothy)

Drafted by Fuller Feuds Dev, Nov 20 2026. Twenty broad, evergreen topics for the founding members to open with.
Titles are neutral questions; descriptions give both sides one line each. Published to production on Nov 20 2026
as @timf with no opinions; the first opinions should come from Timothy or the founders (not a bot).

Categories reuse the site's existing names where they fit (Politics, Technology, Science, Economics, Sports,
Entertainment, Health, Society, Law, Climate, Education). "Ethics", "Philosophy" and "Religion" would be new
categories (they'd show with the default icon until one is added).

| # | Title | Short description | Categories |
|---|-------|-------------------|------------|
| 1 | Should the U.S. use ranked-choice voting in federal elections? | Supporters say it rewards broad appeal and ends "spoiler" fears; critics say it's confusing, slower to count and can still produce odd results. | Politics |
| 2 | Should legal immigration to the U.S. be increased? | One side points to labor shortages, growth and an aging population; the other to wages, housing, integration and the need to fix the system first. | Politics, Economics |
| 3 | Should members of Congress have term limits? | Fresh blood and less entrenched power, or the loss of experience and more influence for lobbyists and staff? | Politics |
| 4 | Should every gun sale require a background check? | Closing private-sale gaps could keep guns from prohibited buyers; opponents say it burdens law-abiding owners and is hard to enforce without a registry. | Law, Politics |
| 5 | Should advanced AI development be slowed until safety rules exist? | Some argue the risks are too big to race ahead; others say pausing just hands the lead to less careful actors and delays real benefits. | Technology |
| 6 | Should social media be banned for kids under 16? | Evidence on teen mental health and online harms versus parental choice, free expression and whether age checks can work without hurting privacy. | Technology, Society |
| 7 | Should AI-generated art be eligible for copyright? | If a person directs the work, should they own it? Or does protection belong only to human creativity, and to the artists whose work trained the models? | Technology, Entertainment, Law |
| 8 | Is it ethical to eat meat? | Animal welfare and climate impact on one side; nutrition, culture, livelihoods and humane farming on the other. | Ethics, Health |
| 9 | Should assisted dying be legal for terminally ill adults? | Autonomy and relief from suffering, or risks of pressure on vulnerable people and a slippery slope in who qualifies? | Ethics, Health, Law |
| 10 | Does God exist? | The oldest debate there is: arguments from design, morality and experience versus the problem of evil, divine hiddenness and naturalism. | Religion, Philosophy |
| 11 | Do humans have free will? | Are our choices truly ours, or the product of causes we never chose, and does the answer change how we should judge people? | Philosophy |
| 12 | Can you separate the art from the artist? | Should a creator's conduct change how we value or support their work, or does good work stand on its own? | Entertainment, Society |
| 13 | Does "cancel culture" do more harm than good? | Accountability for real wrongdoing, or public shaming without due process that chills honest speech? | Society |
| 14 | Should college athletes be paid as employees? | They generate billions for schools; others worry about the end of amateur sport and smaller programs that can't afford salaries. | Sports, Education |
| 15 | Should performance-enhancing drugs be allowed in pro sports? | Legalize and regulate for safety and honesty, or protect fair competition and athletes' long-term health with a ban? | Sports, Health |
| 16 | Is nuclear power the best way to cut carbon emissions? | Reliable zero-carbon power at scale, or too slow, costly and risky compared with renewables and storage? | Science, Climate |
| 17 | Should we bring back extinct species? | De-extinction could restore ecosystems and advance science; critics call it a costly distraction from saving species alive today. | Science |
| 18 | Should the very rich pay an annual wealth tax? | Reducing extreme inequality and funding public goods, or capital flight, valuation headaches and a tax that has struggled where tried? | Economics, Politics |
| 19 | Should the four-day work week become standard? | Trials report similar output and happier staff; skeptics say it doesn't fit many jobs and could raise costs. | Economics, Business |
| 20 | Is college still worth the cost? | Higher lifetime earnings and broader opportunity, or rising debt, credential inflation and better paths through trades and self-teaching? | Education, Economics |

Mix: Politics/Law 4 · Tech & AI 3 · Ethics 2 · Philosophy & religion 2 · Culture 2 · Sports 2 · Science/Climate 2 ·
Economics 2 · Lifestyle/Education 1. Existing topics (e.g. universal basic income, legalization of intoxicating
substances) were left out to avoid duplicates.
